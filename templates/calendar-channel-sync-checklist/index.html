Frequently asked questions

Is this Excel template free to download?+

Yes. Download the .xlsx workbook directly without entering an email address or creating an account. It includes instructions and fictional examples.

Does this workbook connect to Airbnb or Booking.com?+

No. It is a manual checklist for calendar mapping, testing both directions and logging availability checks. It does not connect to channels or prevent double bookings.

Is iCal sync real-time or a full channel manager?+

No. The checklist explains those limits and links to Airbnb and Booking.com guidance. Verify availability in each channel using your normal operating procedures.

Should I paste private calendar feed URLs into the file?+

No. Keep private calendar URLs and credentials out of the workbook. Use references to a secure system instead.

Does sample data affect my real results?+

Examples are on a separate sheet and do not enter your live results.

Has it been tested in Microsoft Excel?+

It is supplied as an Excel .xlsx file and was recalculated and tested in LibreOffice Calc, not native desktop Microsoft Excel. Check it in your spreadsheet app before relying on the results. Formula cells are not locked.
