Frequently asked questions

Is this Excel template free to download?+

Yes. Download the .xlsx workbook directly without entering an email address or creating an account. It includes instructions and fictional examples.

What does the turnover checklist include?+

A reusable task list with assigned owners, status, checked dates and short issue references. Completion is calculated from the live Checklist sheet, not the Example sheet.

How does the checklist treat N/A tasks?+

N/A tasks are excluded from the completion percentage. Blank statuses count as unfinished. Blank task rows are ignored.

Does completing it certify that a property is safe or ready?+

No. It is not a safety inspection, cleaning certification or guarantee of readiness. Adapt it to the property and your professional procedures.

Does sample data affect my real results?+

Examples are on a separate sheet and do not enter your live results.

Has it been tested in Microsoft Excel?+

It is supplied as an Excel .xlsx file and was recalculated and tested in LibreOffice Calc, not native desktop Microsoft Excel. Check it in your spreadsheet app before relying on the results. Formula cells are not locked.
