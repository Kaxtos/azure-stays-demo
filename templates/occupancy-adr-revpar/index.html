Frequently asked questions

Is this Excel template free to download?+

Yes. Download the .xlsx workbook directly without entering an email address or creating an account. It includes instructions and fictional examples.

How are occupancy, ADR and RevPAR calculated?+

Occupancy is paid occupied unit-nights divided by available unit-nights. ADR is accommodation revenue divided by paid occupied unit-nights. RevPAR is accommodation revenue divided by available unit-nights. A missing denominator leaves the rate blank.

Which revenue and nights should I enter?+

Use accommodation revenue net of accommodation refunds, excluding cleaning, other fees and taxes. Enter calendar unit-nights and unavailable nights using a consistent availability policy.

Does it split cross-month stays automatically?+

No. Split nights and revenue across months manually. This is an operational tracker, not an STR reporting submission.

Does sample data affect my real results?+

Sample rows are excluded in LIVE mode.

Has it been tested in Microsoft Excel?+

It is supplied as an Excel .xlsx file and was recalculated and tested in LibreOffice Calc, not native desktop Microsoft Excel. Check it in your spreadsheet app before relying on the results. Formula cells are not locked.
