ABOUT THIS WORKBOOK

Frequently asked questions

Is this Excel template free to download?+

Yes. Download the .xlsx workbook directly without entering an email address or creating an account. It includes instructions and fictional examples.

How do I use this guest handover checklist?+

Save one copy per guest stay, complete the property code, reference and planned date, then assign arrival and departure tasks and record status and checked dates.

Does this cover guest identity or registration compliance?+

No. It is an operational handover checklist, not a legal, registration, accessibility or identity-check compliance form. Confirm applicable requirements separately.

Should I store guest names, passport details or access codes here?+

No. Use references to your secure system. Do not store guest names, passport data, access codes or passwords in the workbook.

Does sample data affect my real results?+

Examples are on a separate sheet and do not enter your live results.

Has it been tested in Microsoft Excel?+

It is supplied as an Excel .xlsx file and was recalculated and tested in LibreOffice Calc, not native desktop Microsoft Excel. Check it in your spreadsheet app before relying on the results. Formula cells are not locked.
