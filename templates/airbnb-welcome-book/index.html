Frequently asked questions

Is this welcome book template free?+

Yes. Download the .docx directly without an email address or account, then edit it for your property.

Which apps open the file?+

Microsoft Word, LibreOffice Writer and Google Docs all open .docx. Export to PDF when you want a fixed layout for printing or sharing.

Can I change everything?+

Yes. The document is fully editable. Replace every [bracketed] field and rewrite the sample lines so they match your property.

Does it cover legal house rules?+

No. The text is placeholder only and not legal, safety or insurance advice. Adapt it to your property and check local requirements.

Why bother with a welcome book?+

Guests find Wi-Fi, rules and local tips in one place, which cuts repeated questions and sets expectations before arrival.
