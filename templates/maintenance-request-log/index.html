Frequently asked questions

Is this Excel template free to download?+

Yes. Download the .xlsx workbook directly without entering an email address or creating an account. It includes instructions and fictional examples.

What can I track in the maintenance request log?+

Request references, property codes, priority, assigned owner, due date, status, estimated cost and actual cost. The summary counts open and overdue requests using your entered as-of date.

How does it decide whether a request is open or overdue?+

Open means a nonblank request ID with a status other than Closed or Cancelled. Age and overdue checks remain blank until you enter an as-of date. Check the data warnings before using the summary.

Is it an emergency system or does it charge owners?+

No. Urgent hazards need your established emergency procedure. It is not an emergency service or safety inspection, and no entered cost is automatically charged to an owner.

Does sample data affect my real results?+

Sample rows are excluded in LIVE mode.

Has it been tested in Microsoft Excel?+

It is supplied as an Excel .xlsx file and was recalculated and tested in LibreOffice Calc, not native desktop Microsoft Excel. Check it in your spreadsheet app before relying on the results. Formula cells are not locked.
