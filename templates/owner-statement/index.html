Frequently asked questions

Is this Excel template free to download?+

Yes. Download the .xlsx workbook directly without entering an email address or creating an account. It includes instructions and fictional examples.

What does this owner statement template calculate?+

It brings accommodation income, cleaning and other income, refunds, platform fees, commission, owner charges, opening balance and prior payments into a monthly payout statement for the selected owner and property.

What is the management commission based on?+

The commission is applied to accommodation income less accommodation refunds only. Check this matches your agreed owner terms before using the statement.

Does it split bookings across months or calculate VAT?+

No. Income falls in the entered posting month. There is no VAT, tax or withholding calculation; reconcile the statement with your accounting records.

Does sample data affect my real results?+

Sample rows are excluded in default LIVE mode. Use LIVE mode for your own records.

Has it been tested in Microsoft Excel?+

It is supplied as an Excel .xlsx file and was recalculated and tested in LibreOffice Calc, not native desktop Microsoft Excel. Check it in your spreadsheet app before relying on the results. Formula cells are not locked.
