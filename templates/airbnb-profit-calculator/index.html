ABOUT THIS TEMPLATE

Frequently asked questions

Is this profit calculator free?+

Yes. Download the .xlsx workbook directly without an email address or account. It includes instructions and a fictional example.

Does it include the Airbnb host fee?+

Yes. Enter the rate your listing actually pays: Airbnb uses either a split fee (about 3% from the host) or a host-only fee (often 15% or more for software-connected listings). The workbook applies it to accommodation plus guest fees.

Is the result my real profit?+

No. It is a monthly operating estimate before loan principal, income tax, VAT and one-off purchases. It is not an accounting or tax figure and not a promise of income.

Where do I find my real numbers?+

Your platform payout breakdown shows nightly amounts, guest fees and host fees. Your bank or card statements show cleaning, laundry, utilities and maintenance costs.

Has it been tested in Microsoft Excel?+

It is supplied as an Excel .xlsx file and was recalculated and tested in LibreOffice Calc, not native desktop Microsoft Excel. Check it in your spreadsheet app before relying on the results. Formula cells are not locked.
